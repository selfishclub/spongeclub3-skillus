## Lifecycle phase definitions (shared vocabulary)

- **GA (General Availability):** Product is actively sold and fully supported
- **NSC (Notice of Status Change):** Internal decision communicated; planning begins
- **EOS (End of Sale):** No new customers can purchase
- **EOE (End of Expansion):** Existing customers cannot add capacity/seats
- **EOR (End of Renewal):** Existing contracts will not be renewed
- **EOM (End of Maintenance):** Bug fixes and patches stop
- **EOL (End of Life):** Product is fully retired
- **EOSRV (End of Service):** All support and service obligations end

## Gotchas and things to watch

1. **Tier consistency:** Use identical tier-definition text across all 6 skills (extract from
   the table above). Different tier classifications for the same situation = a bug.
2. **Example coherence:** The Fieldlight and NFA-200 stories span 10 example files. Define
   dates, customer counts, and replacement names once and use them consistently.
3. **Anonymization:** The workshop decks reference a real client and a real product line. Do not
   carry either forward — use the fictional universes. Do not restate the specifics here.
4. **Description length:** YAML `description` must be <=200 chars and trigger-oriented ("Use
   when..."). Quote values containing colons.
5. **Interactive skills are SKILL.md only** — no template.md or examples/ directory.
6. **The "Final Step" pattern:** Each prompt in the source repo ends with 4 numbered options
   that cross-link to the other prompts. Replicate this in the skills as numbered next-step
   options referencing the other EOL skills by path.
